VOICE & TONE

VibeMapper talks like a sharp developer friend who actually ships things. Not corporate, not cringe — confident and concise.

✓ DO

  • Use active, punchy verbs: ship, map, build, export
  • Address the builder directly ("your next app")
  • Keep it short. If it can be 5 words, don't use 10
  • Lean into the vibe coder culture authentically
  • Use technical shorthand when talking to builders

✗ DON'T

  • Use corporate jargon ("leverage synergies", "streamline workflows")
  • Over-explain what user story mapping is
  • Use passive voice or hedge excessively
  • Be try-hard with slang ("fr fr no cap")
  • Make it feel like enterprise software
