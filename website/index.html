SEE YOUR APP
BEFORE IT EXISTS

AI STORY GENERATION

Describe your app in plain words. VibMapper generates a visual map of every activity, step, and story — so you can see the whole product before writing any code.

VISUAL CANVAS

Your app's blueprint on an infinite zoomable canvas. Skim the entire product in one glance — no scrolling through endless docs. Drag, edit, rearrange until it matches your vision.

AI MAP EDITING

Tell AI what to change and it surgically updates your map. Diff highlighting shows exactly what was added or modified — like a PR review for your product plan.

PRIORITY SWIM LANES

Slice your vision into Must-have, Should-have, Could-have, and Won't-have. See your MVP scope at a glance — know exactly what to build first and what to skip.

MCP SERVER

Claude Code is the brain, VibMapper is the memory. Connect via MCP and your coding agent can read the plan, update progress, and stay aligned with your vision as it builds.

SHARE & COLLABORATE

Invite teammates with viewer or editor roles. Share read-only links for feedback. Version history lets you snapshot and restore any state of your product plan.

# Add VibMapper to Claude Code

  -e USM_API_TOKEN=mcp_your_token \
